ARIA pattern mocks

For the phase 9 plan, decisions 5 to 7. Light theme only; dark follows the existing tokens. Interface copy is Polish, as in the app. In 1B the accordion works: Tab to a header and press Enter or Space, and the log and the ARIA table follow.

Native verdict glyphs used throughout: ● Wystarczy HTML, ◐ HTML z dodatkiem ARIA, ○ Tylko ARIA. The shape and the words carry the meaning, never colour.

1. Pattern page, /wzorce/akordeon-pojedynczy

Decision 5. Same content in all three; what changes is where the log sits and whether the page leads the reader through the keys.

1A Stacked: the pattern, then three tables in a row

The frame gets the full width. Log, keys and ARIA sit under it side by side, so they are out of view while the reader presses keys on a short screen.

1C Exercise: the keyboard table becomes a checklist

Each keyboard row turns into a step with what the reader should hear. The frame ticks a step when it sees the matching key and announcement. Teaches more, costs more: steps need an expected announcement per row in the frontmatter. Layout otherwise as 1B.
