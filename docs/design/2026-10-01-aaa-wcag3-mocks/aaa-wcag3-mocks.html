2. The WCAG 3.0 page, /wcag-3

The badge with a double rule is new: it means "W3C Working Draft" and nothing else, so it cannot be confused with the dashed "szkic" badge for our unverified content. Every variant shows the status, the comparison with 2.x and the mapping; they differ in how the mapping is laid out.

2A One page, groups as sections

Status and comparison on top, then the 12 groups as sections with an index on the right. Each guideline is a stacked row with its own anchor, so criterion pages can link straight to it. Two groups shown, the rest collapsed in the mock only.
