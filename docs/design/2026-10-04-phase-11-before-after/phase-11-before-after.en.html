1. What AU does and what we take

AU 3.0 has four pages: an index, the inaccessible home page of a fictional university, a list of 18 issues with a solution for each, and the accessible home page. The two home pages look almost the same. Most fixes live in the markup, which is the point.

The index tells the reader to go in this order: find as many problems as you can on your own, compare with the list, then look at the fixed page. We keep that order.

AU's 18 issues are headings, alt text, decorative images, contrast, a hover-only dropdown, focus, "click here" links, colour, language, form labels, CAPTCHA, validation, landmarks, a modal, a carousel, a data table, abbreviations and HTML validity. We drop two of them. HTML validity was 4.1.1, which WCAG 2.2 removed, and abbreviations are 3.1.4, an AAA criterion. We add three that WCAG 2.1 and 2.2 brought: reflow (1.4.10), target size (2.5.8) and focus not obscured (2.4.11).

The app already has twelve examples, each showing one fault on its own, and 30 patterns. What it does not have is a page where the faults sit together the way they do on a real site, and where the reader can look for them before reading the answer.

2. The topic

Komunikacja Miejska Wrzosów (KMW): the home page of the transport operator of a fictional city. Everyone uses public transport, including people who cannot drive, so the stakes are easy to explain. The subject brings the widgets on its own: a timetable table, a live departure board, disruption alerts, a complaint form, and a box in Ukrainian, which Polish operators really publish. AU is a university and the phase 10 example is a municipal page, so neither repeats. Alternatives are in decision 1.

The page, top to bottom:

  1. A modal "Utrudnienia w ruchu" that opens on load.
  2. The header: logo, social media icons, a "Українською" link.
  3. The main menu with submenus: Rozkłady, Bilety, Utrudnienia, O nas.
  4. A carousel of announcements.
  5. "Odjazdy z przystanku Rynek", a departure board that refreshes itself.
  6. "Aktualności", three news items.
  7. An image of the network map.
  8. "Інформація для пасажирів", a box in Ukrainian.
  9. The form "Zgłoś uwagę": name, email, line, date, type of report (radio buttons), message, consent, CAPTCHA.
  10. The footer, and a cookie bar fixed to the bottom of the window.

4. What we build

  1. Two pages, broken and fixed, as standalone documents at /demo/przed-i-po/przed and /demo/przed-i-po/po, prerendered like the example demos. How the reader sees them: mock section 2.
  2. An entry page at /praktyka/przed-i-po, with the three steps in AU's order. Steps 2 and 3 open once the reader finishes checking. Where it sits in the app: mock section 1.
  3. Notes the reader takes while checking the broken page, kept in the browser (section 5, mock section 2).
  4. The list of problems, shown after the check, with the reader's notes beside it: for each problem, what is wrong, who it hurts, how to fix it, the code before and after, and links to the criteria and to the example or pattern that shows it alone. Mock sections 3 and 4.
  5. Markers: after the check, a switch "Znaczniki problemów" puts numbered badges on the broken page, each linking to its item in the list. During the check the switch does not exist.
  6. Links back: a criterion page that has a problem on the page says "Na stronie przed i po: problem 7", and so do the examples and patterns from the last column of section 3.
  7. Search indexes the entry page and the twenty problems.

5. Self-check: notes and hidden answers

The reader checks the broken page alone. Anything that gives the answers away stays hidden until they say they are done, and the notes they took are waiting for them next to the list.

Two stages

StageThe reader hasHidden
Checking (the default)The broken page, the notes, the motion switch, the number 20 as a goalThe list, the markers, the fixed page, every hint of where the problems are
After the checkEverything: the list with the notes beside it, markers on the broken page, the fixed pageNothing

"Kończę sprawdzanie", in the notes panel and on the entry page, moves to the second stage. "Zacznij od nowa" deletes the notes and hides everything again, after a confirmation that says the notes will be deleted.

The lock is soft. The list and the fixed page have public URLs, and the entry page and the demo bar simply do not link to them during the check. Opening the list directly shows "Najpierw sprawdź stronę sam" with a "Pokaż listę mimo to" link, which ends the check. Nobody gains anything by cheating, so nothing more is needed.

Links back from criterion pages, examples and patterns ("Na stronie przed i po: problem 7") stay visible at all times. They sit on other pages, off the path of someone checking, and hiding them would mean client state on prerendered pages.

No answers in the source

A reader who opens developer tools on the broken page should not find the answers there either. przed.html carries the data-problem attributes the markers need, so the build serves two documents from it:

  • /demo/przed-i-po/przed, with no data-problem and no HTML comments, for the check;
  • /demo/przed-i-po/przed-znaczniki, with both and with the marker script, linked only after the check.

Class names and ids in przed.html are neutral (.menu, not .menu-hover-only), and so is the page title. A test searches the check document for words like "problem", "błąd" and "a11y".

Notes

  • Free text, one note per thing the reader noticed: a field "Co nie działa?" and a button "Dodaj notatkę", with the notes listed below it, each with "Edytuj" and "Usuń". No picker for the region or the criterion, since either would hint at the answer.
  • Notes keep their order and the time they were added.
  • They live in the browser next to the progress (decision 11), so they survive closing the tab. With storage blocked they last until the tab closes and the panel says so, as /sciezki does.
  • "Kopiuj notatki" puts them on the clipboard as plain text, for a trainer collecting results (decision 12).
  • Where the panel sits while checking: mock section 2. How the notes meet the list afterwards: mock section 3, decision 4.

The fixed page is the end result

po has no markers, no notes and no motion switch. Its carousel starts still and has its own pause button, as a correct page should. Its demo bar says "Wersja poprawiona: tak ta strona powinna wyglądać" and links to the list and back to the app. It is linked only after the check.

6. Content format

content/przed-i-po/
  index.md                  intro for the entry page, status, lastVerified
  przed.html                the broken page: markup, <style>, <script>
  po.html                   the fixed page
  problemy/
    01-jezyk.md
    02-punkty-orientacyjne.md
    ...
    20-stala-szerokosc.md

One file per problem keeps a diff readable and lets a problem be verified on its own, like criteria. Frontmatter:

title: Menu rozwija się tylko pod myszą
criteria: ["2.1.1", "4.1.2"]
marker: menu            # data-problem="menu" in przed.html
who: [klawiatura, czytnik]
axe: []                 # rules axe must report on przed.html
example: null
pattern: nawigacja-wielopoziomowa
status: szkic

The body has ## Problem and ## Rozwiązanie, with code blocks tagged przed and po. A new src/content/before-after.ts parses it with an ArkType schema, in the shape of examples.ts: the number comes from the file name, the types are inferred from the schema, and a parse error names the file and the reason.

Checks at build time, with a test for each:

  • Numbers run from 1 with no gaps.
  • Every marker appears exactly once as data-problem in przed.html, and no data-problem is left without a problem.
  • Criteria, example and pattern slugs exist.

7. Motion and a page that is broken on purpose

Problems 9 and 13 are motion: a carousel that turns and a board that refreshes on its own. The app opts into 2.3.3 and starts moving demos only when the reader asks. So the broken page loads still, and a "Włącz ruch" switch starts both. That is the current rule for examples, applied to a whole page. With prefers-reduced-motion the switch still works, since the reader chose it, but slides change without a transition.

The modal (problem 3) opens on load, because that is the fault. It is not a keyboard trap: focus goes behind it, which is the fault we show. A real trap (2.1.2) would lock a keyboard user inside a demo, so we do not build one.

8. Accessibility of everything around the broken page

  • Both pages start with a demo bar that is not part of KMW, and it is the first stop for Tab. On the broken page during the check it says "Wersja zepsuta: ta strona celowo łamie zasady dostępności. Część rzeczy nie zadziała z klawiatury ani z czytnikiem ekranu." and offers the notes, the motion switch, "Kończę sprawdzanie" and a way back to the app. After the check it adds the list, the markers switch and the fixed page. On the fixed page it offers the list and the way back. It is accessible in both versions.
  • The notes panel is a labelled field, a real button, and a list with an announced count ("3 notatki"). Adding a note keeps focus in the field, so the reader can type the next one; deleting one moves focus to the next note, or to the field when none is left.
  • The bar is a named aside and is not sticky, so it never covers focus the way problem 19 does.
  • The entry page and the list are app pages and follow docs/accessibility.md like every other page.
  • The entry page warns before the link to the broken page, as AU does, so nobody walks into it unprepared.

9. Tests

  • Unit (Vitest): the parser rejects bad frontmatter, and the checks from section 6. The store keeps notes and revealedAt, reads a stored object without reviews, and "Zacznij od nowa" clears both.
  • No answers in the source: the check document has no data-problem, no comments and none of the giveaway words.
  • The two stages (Playwright): during the check, no link on the entry page or the demo bar leads to the list, the markers or the fixed page; a note survives a reload; "Kończę sprawdzanie" shows the list with the notes beside it.
  • axe (Playwright): przed reports every rule its problems list, and only those rules; po reports nothing; the demo bar reports nothing in either version.
  • Keyboard on po: the menu opens with Enter and closes with Esc; the modal takes focus and gives it back; the carousel pauses; after a bad submit, focus is on the error summary and its links reach the fields; the focused element is never under the cookie bar; no horizontal scroll at 320 pixels.

Nothing tests that the faults in przed still work beyond what axe sees. Those tests would only stop a fix nobody plans to make.

11. Decisions

  1. Topic. Komunikacja Miejska Wrzosów (recommended). Alternatives: a public library (catalogue search, reader card form, events) or a clinic's online registration (appointment booking, opening hours).
  2. Where it lives. Mock section 1: a third tab in Praktyka, or a card above the examples.
  3. How the broken page opens and where the notes sit. Mock section 2: a full window with a notes drawer in the demo bar, a frame in the app with the notes under the toolbar, or a frame with the notes in a rail beside it. Recommended: 2C. The notes stay in view while the reader looks, everything lives in React, and the frame brings the simulators almost for free. 2A needs the store again as a separate bundle inside the demo document, kept in step with the app through storage events.
  4. How the notes meet the list. Mock section 3: the notes above the list (3A), or side by side with "Znalazłem" on every problem and "Znalazłeś 7 z 20" (3B). Recommended: 3A. The reader compares on their own, and a score invites arguing over what counts as a match.
  5. List layout. Mock section 4: a long list like AU, a table you can filter by who is affected, or a map of the page with the list grouped by region.
  6. The look of KMW. Mock section 5: a municipal portal from around 2012, or a modern site. The modern one makes a point AU cannot: a site can look new and still fail.
  7. The fixed widgets. Write compact code for po.html following the phase 9 patterns, and link to them (recommended), or load the pattern files themselves. Pattern files carry hooks for the live log, which this page does not need.
  8. How many problems. Twenty (recommended) or fourteen, dropping 5, 8, 12, 15, 19 and 20, which the examples already show on their own.
  9. CAPTCHA in the fixed version. Remove it and explain the alternatives (a honeypot field, server-side limits), which is recommended, or replace it with a text question as AU does.
  10. A lesson. "Przegląd całej strony" on the tester path with a quiz built on this page. Recommended: a later phase.
  11. Where notes are stored. In the progress object under reviews (recommended: phase 5 imports them with everything else), or under a storage key of their own.
  12. "Kopiuj notatki". Yes, one button (recommended), or no.

12. Out of scope

  • A second site or KMW subpages. Menu links go nowhere and say so.
  • Video and captions. AU has them on a separate page; ours would be a separate phase.
  • An English version of KMW.
  • Simulators on the full window version (decision 3 brings them in the frame variants).
