Cookie consent banner: variants A, B, C

Google Analytics G-2DGTWE359W with Consent Mode v2, default denied. Each variant: desktop in light theme, 360 px in dark theme. Colours from src/styles.css. Shared rules: the banner is a labelled region, not a modal, so focus is never trapped; "Odrzuć" and "Zgadzam się" look identical; targets are at least 44×44 px; no animation; after a choice focus moves to #main; the choice lives in localStorage. The focus ring on "Odrzuć" shows 2.4.13.

A. Sticky bar at the bottom

The most familiar pattern. It covers the bottom of the viewport, so while it is open "Struktura strony" moves above it and scroll-padding-bottom grows by the bar height (2.4.11).

B. Card in the bottom-left corner

Opposite corner from "Struktura strony". Covers the least content on desktop. On a phone it becomes full width and takes about a third of the screen; "Struktura strony" hides until the choice is made.

C. Bar above the header, in normal flow (recommended)

Pushes the page down instead of covering it, adds nothing sticky, so 2.4.11 is not at risk. It sits right after the skip link: first in Tab order and reading order. After a choice it disappears and focus moves to #main.

Shared: footer with a way back to the choice

The layout has no footer today. Withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it, so every variant needs a permanent "Ustawienia cookies" control. It is a button that shows the banner again, with the current choice stated in the text.
