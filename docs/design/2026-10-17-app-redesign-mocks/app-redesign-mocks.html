Mocks: app redesign, seven variants

Plan: docs/design/2026-10-17-app-redesign/app-redesign.en.html (Polish: app-redesign.pl.html). Brief: simple and intuitive, fun to look at with motion that says "playground", content found fast, built for neurodivergent readers and people using assistive tech. Tone: playful for A, B and C; D and E, added on request, are the more professional end. Each variant is a whole design system, shown on the home page, a criterion page (/kryteria/1.4.3) and a phone.

The toggles above each variant are real: "Spokój" (calm mode) stops every animation and drops the decorative colour, the second one flips light and dark. The labels "Spokój" and "Tryb ciemny" inside the mocks drive the same toggles. Hover the tiles and buttons, and try the contrast toy in A.

A · final v2: Piaskownica, own system

  • Replaces "A · final" below, which followed PostHog too closely. PostHog is kept only as principles: a calm ground, white cards, hierarchy by weight, a small set of callouts. Colours, type and layout are new and locked in DESIGN.md.
  • Seven toy colours, one per kind of content: sky (Kryteria), slide (Praktyka), grass (Ścieżki), plum (Prawo), lollipop (Symulatory), sun (Słownik), sea (WCAG 3.0). Text on every colour is ink at 9:1 or more; the deep shade of each colour draws its icons.
  • The signature is the xylophone: the seven sections as keys of falling length on the home page, and a small three-key mark next to the name that plays on hover.
  • Criterion pages open on a full-width band in the kind's colour; sections are cards with coloured tabs; a "pocket" card keeps the contents and the facts in reach.

F and G, further down, are built from the concept image you sent, with its six favicon ideas redrawn at the end.

A · final: Piaskownica on PostHog DNA (superseded by v2)

  • The picked variant A, rebuilt on the system studied at shadcn.io/design/posthog. Superseded on 2026-10-17 by v2 above, which uses its own colours and layout.
  • From PostHog: cream canvas and white cards, IBM Plex Sans with hierarchy by weight, one saturated colour (orange) kept for the primary action, hairline and dashed rules, four pastel callouts, dark code islands, a footer of index columns.
  • From A: the bento of seven sections, the contrast toy, the hopping ball on the wordmark, the six fixes that pay off most.
  • Changed for accessibility: body text darkened to #44463e and links to #1e40af to stay at 7:1; the faint blue focus ring replaced by a solid 3 px outline; a dark mode added (PostHog has none, the app does).

A · Piaskownica

  • Warm and bright. Every section of the app gets its own tint, icon and name, so tiles never rely on colour alone.
  • The home page puts search first, then a bento of the seven sections, then the six fixes that pay off most.
  • The playground lives in small toys: the contrast swing on the home page, the before/after switch on criterion pages.

B · Mapa placu

  • The home page is a plan of the playground: seven places joined by chalk paths. It is an ordered list of links in the code, so a screen reader and 400% zoom get a plain list.
  • One side rail on every page keeps the seven places, search and settings in the same spot. On a criterion page "Na tej stronie" opens inside the rail, so there is one place to look.
  • Criterion pages are a route with numbered stops. Neurodivergent readers see how long the page is and where they are.

C · Klocki

  • Toy bricks at dusk. Dark is the first look, light is equal; the toggle flips it. Every brick keeps its name and count, colour is a bonus.
  • The home page is an index: a big search box, type bricks that filter it, then every entry point in four columns. The fastest variant for someone who knows what they want.
  • Progress is a tower of bricks per path. A finished lesson drops a brick on top. Criterion pages are building instructions with numbered steps.

D · Pracownia

  • The professional end: the calm of developer documentation (Stripe, Linear) with one deep coral accent. Grey paper, thin rules, 8 px corners.
  • The home page is a workbench: a command search (Ctrl K), one live component with what a screen reader says, then the sections as a table with counts.
  • Criterion pages use a documentation layout: the tree of every criterion on the left, properties in a table, examples with "Podgląd / Kod" tabs, "Na tej stronie" on the right.
  • The playground stays in the working examples, not in the decoration.

E · Przewodnik

  • The public-service end, after GOV.UK and the US Web Design System: one column, big plain links, a serif for headings, Public Sans for text. Reads like an official guide, which suits public bodies, the people who most often have a legal duty.
  • The home page starts from a task, not a section: "Zacznij od swojej roli", then the questions people actually ask, then every section with one line each.
  • Criterion pages read top to bottom: summary in an inset, contents at the top, the example with "Źle / Dobrze" labels. Almost no motion: link underlines thicken on hover, that is all.
  • Focus is a yellow fill with a thick ink bar, the best-known focus style in public services.

F · Plac zabaw (from your concept)

  • Built from the concept you sent: split hero with the three-line headline, the playground illustration with a code card, four playground cards, a closing strip. Light first, dark equal. In dark mode the primary colour turns from indigo to mint, as in the concept.
  • Kept: the logo idea 1 (A from two strokes, play triangle), the multi-colour shapes, the indigo / mint pair, rounded cards with tags.
  • Changed for the brief and for accessibility: Polish copy; no pill above the headline; the second line is one solid colour, not a gradient; the code card has no fake window dots; "Accessibility score 92" is replaced by a real number (the contrast of the button right next to it, 8.2:1); no "real-time feedback" claim, because the app has none.
  • The illustration is drawn in SVG, decorative and hidden from screen readers. A final version could use a commissioned 3D illustration like the concept's; the layout holds either way.

G · Plac wieczorem (concept, adapted)

  • The concept's dark screen as the first look, reshaped for the brief: search comes before the illustration, because finding content fast was the main ask.
  • The checklist card from the concept ("Test. Learn. Improve.") is real: tick what you have checked on your own site; each item links to the lesson that teaches it. It is the one toy on the page.
  • Below the playground cards the paths show progress, like today's progress store. The toggle here switches to light.

Favicon concepts from the image

The six ideas redrawn as SVG, each at 96 px and at 16, 32 and 48 px on a light and a dark browser tab. At 16 px only the simplest survive: 1 and 3 stay readable, 6 reads as a ring, 2 as a coloured circle, 5 loses its text, 4 turns into a coloured blot.

Shared by every variant

  • Spokój in the header of every page: stops all motion, drops decorative tints, stays on across visits. It starts on when the system asks for reduced motion.
  • Search on every page with the / shortcut. The seven sections keep one icon, one name and one colour across the app.
  • Kept from today: skip link, "Struktura strony", the docked phone navigation, the consent card, the 3px focus outline, monospace for criterion and article numbers, and one meaning for the marker colour: what the user gains.
  • Text pairs stay at 7:1 or more in both modes, targets at 44px or more. D and E keep the same rules; their calm mode only has a little to switch off. F and G hide the decorative shapes in calm mode.
