Mocks: Open Graph image and favicon

Belongs to the SEO and indexing plan, commits 6 to 8. 2026-10-10. Pick one OG variant (A, B, C) and one favicon (1, 2, 3).

All three OG variants use the app's own tokens: paper #fbfaf7, ink #14171a, indigo #2c36a8, the marker yellow #f4e75b. Each is drawn with flexbox, borders and plain text only, which is what satori renders. Text pairs reach at least 7:1. The image always repeats the page title, so og:image:alt can be the title and the image carries nothing the text does not.

Open Graph image, 1200×630

A · Zakreślacz

Paper, like the app. The page's identifier sits on the yellow marker, the way the app marks what a user gains. Quiet, reads as a page of the site.

B · Fokus

Ink background, the title inside a thick yellow outline drawn like the app's focus indicator (2.4.13). The most distinctive in a feed, and the outline is the subject itself: where focus is, you can see it.

C · Numer

The number people search for (1.4.3, Art. 5) gets its own ink column, set large in yellow mono. Pages without a number (home, lists) show the section name there instead. Best for criteria, weakest for long article titles.

Favicon

SVG for browsers plus a 180 px PNG for Apple touch icons. Shown at 16, 32 and 64 px on light and dark, and in a browser tab. A favicon has to survive 16 px, so each one is a single shape and at most two letters.

Notes for the build

  • The SVG favicon will be drawn as paths, not <text>, so it does not depend on fonts installed on the reader's machine.
  • The mono parts of A, B and C need a second font file for satori (JetBrains Mono, OFL) next to Inter. Without it they fall back to Inter.
  • My pick: B with favicon 1. They share the focus ring, an image taken straight from the subject, and B stands out in both a light and a dark feed. A is the safe choice if you want the preview to look like the app page.
