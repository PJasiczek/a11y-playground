Home screen: three directions

Same content, three ideas about what the home screen is for. Shared across all three: Inter for interface text, a monospace face reserved for criterion numbers and dates because those are codes, and one yellow marker highlight as the signature, borrowed from marking up a printed statute. Interface copy is Polish. Headings inside the mocks start at h3 so this comparison page keeps a valid outline; in the app they would be h1. The wordmark sets a11y in the monospace face, since the 11 is a numeronym and belongs with the other codes.

A — Trzy drzwi

A question-led opening plus search, then exactly three doors: criteria, law, practice. Assumes most visits start with "I do not know where to look". Safest, most explanatory.

B — Katalog od razu

No hero at all. The home screen is the criteria index with filters, and search sits in the top bar. The boldest simplification: treats the app as a tool you return to, not a site you are introduced to. Resume line sits above the list, one row tall.
